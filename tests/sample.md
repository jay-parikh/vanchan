## Chapter One: Habits

### The first hour

The main message is simple. If you protect the first hour of your day
and use it well, the rest of the day gets better.

1. Move your body for twenty minutes, hard enough to sweat a little bit today.
2. Plan and reflect for twenty minutes with a journal and a quiet mind here.
3. Learn something new for twenty minutes from a good book or a course.
4. Repeat this every day for sixty-six days until it becomes automatic.
5. Track your progress and forgive the days you miss without guilt at all.
